Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 23 March 2026

This privacy policy describes the collection and processing of information about you that can directly or indirectly identify you ("Personal Data") carried out by the "Manga Sketch AR" app, available on Google Play.

1. Core Functionality: Camera Access

Strict Privacy: The App requires access to your device's camera strictly to project AR sketches onto your physical drawing surface. We do not record, save, or transmit your camera feed. All Augmented Reality (AR) processing happens locally on your device in real-time.

2. Which Data Do We Collect and Why?

We respect your privacy and aim to limit the data we collect to what is strictly necessary to maintain and improve the App. Because our App does not require a user account, we do not collect your name, email, or phone number.

A. Analytics and App Performance

To help us improve our App, fix bugs, and understand which features are most popular, we automatically collect anonymous data using Google Analytics and Firebase. The data collected includes:

  • Device Information: Manufacturer, model, operating system, and unique device identifiers.
  • Usage Information: Time, frequency, and duration of activities, and interactions with App features.
  • Crash Reports: Diagnostic data to help us fix bugs and prevent future crashes.

B. Payments and Subscriptions

If you purchase paid features or subscriptions, your payment is processed directly by the respective platform (Google Play Store). We do not collect, process, or store your bank, debit, credit card, or personal billing details.

To manage your subscription status, validate purchases, and ensure you have access to premium content, we use RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives anonymous App User IDs and purchase history metadata directly from the App Stores.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We share your data with the following service providers strictly for analytics, operational, and subscription management purposes:

  • Google Analytics / Firebase: Used for crash reporting and tracking user engagement. (Privacy Policy)
  • RevenueCat: Used to securely manage and validate in-app purchases and subscriptions. (Privacy Policy)

4. Your Data Rights & Deletion

Because Manga Sketch AR does not require you to create an account, all analytics and usage data collected by our third-party services is tied to anonymous, randomly generated device identifiers.

As we do not possess your name, email, or direct contact info, we cannot link this anonymous data to you personally, nor can we locate it in our databases to fulfill manual deletion requests via email.

If you wish to stop all data collection and clear your local data, you simply need to:

  • Uninstall the App from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Use of our App is limited to users aged 13 and above (or 16 depending on your local jurisdiction). We do not intentionally target children or knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. However, our analytics tools collect anonymous device data automatically upon opening the App.

If you are a parent or guardian and realize your child is using the App without your permission, please uninstall the App from their device to instantly cease any local analytics data collection.
