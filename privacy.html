Manga Sketch AR

Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Welcome to Manga Sketch AR, an Android application designed to help artists of all levels master manga drawing through Augmented Reality (AR) tracing.

1. Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2023

We value your privacy and aim for total transparency. Our policy is "Sober": we only ask for the permissions necessary to make the app work.

Camera Access (Core Functionality)

  • Purpose: The app requires access to your camera to project manga sketches onto your physical drawing surface using AR.
  • Privacy: We do not record, save, or transmit your camera feed. The AR processing happens locally on your device hardware in real-time.

Data Collection & Usage

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • Usage Analytics: We collect anonymous technical data (e.g., which categories like Hair or Eyes are most used) to improve the app experience.
  • Crash Reporting: Technical logs are sent in the event of a crash to help us provide a stable experience.

Storage

The app may request permission to save images to your device if you choose to export your progress or save a specific sketch for offline use.

3. Features At a Glance

  • AR Tracing: Use your phone as a digital light box to trace sketches onto paper.
  • Learning Catalog: Specialized categories for:
    • Hair & Eyes
    • Poses & Anatomy
    • Clothing & Pets
    • Mouths & Expressions
  • Progressive Difficulty: Sketches ranging from beginner to advanced levels.
