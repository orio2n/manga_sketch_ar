How to draw anime hands, step by step

To draw anime hands, start with a slightly flat box for the palm and an arc for the knuckles. Add the thumb as a wedge on the side of the palm, then each finger as three short cylinders that get smaller toward the tip, with the middle finger the longest. Bend the segments at their joints for each pose, then draw a simplified outline over the construction.

Why hands are hard, and how to make them easy

A hand has a palm, five fingers, fourteen finger bones and countless poses, so drawing its outline directly is a guessing game. The solution is the same as for the rest of the body: simplify it into volumes you can turn and bend.

Week 8 of the SketchAI program does exactly that: the hand becomes a palm block with small cylinders for the fingers. Once you can draw that construction in any position, the anime outline on top is the easy part.

Anime hand proportions

Check these proportions on your own hand: they hold for almost every style, from realistic seinen to cute shojo. Anime simply slims the fingers or the palm to suit the character.

  • The whole hand is about as long as the face, from the chin to the hairline.
  • The fingers are about as long as the palm, measured on the middle finger.
  • The middle finger is the longest; the index and ring fingers are a little shorter, and the little finger ends near the last joint of the ring finger.
  • The thumb starts low on the side of the palm, near the wrist, and its tip reaches about the first joint of the index finger.
  • Each finger has three segments, each one shorter than the one before.

How to draw anime hands step by step

Start with an open hand seen from the back, the easiest view. Draw everything lightly: the construction will be covered by the final outline.

  1. Step 1Draw the palm as a box

    Draw a slightly flat box, roughly square seen from the front, a little wider at the knuckles than at the wrist. Turn it in space like any box: its top face shows where the back of the hand points.

  2. Step 2Mark the knuckles with an arc

    Draw a curved line across the top of the box where the fingers start, highest at the middle finger and lower toward the little finger. Mark four small points on it for the knuckles.

  3. Step 3Add the thumb

    Draw the base of the thumb as a fleshy wedge on the side of the palm, starting near the wrist. Add two segments that point away from the palm at an angle: the thumb never lines up with the other fingers.

  4. Step 4Draw the fingers in three segments

    From each knuckle, draw three short cylinders that get smaller toward the tip, with a small circle at each joint. Check the lengths: the middle finger is the longest and the fingers together are about as long as the palm.

  5. Step 5Bend the fingers for the pose

    Fingers bend only at their joints and only toward the palm. When they curl, each segment turns a little more than the one before, and the tips describe an arc. Let the fingers spread slightly apart rather than sticking together.

  6. Step 6Connect the wrist and the forearm

    Treat the wrist as a joint, like the elbow: a small sphere between the end of the forearm and the palm block. It lets the hand tilt up, down and sideways without breaking the arm.

  7. Step 7Draw the anime outline

    Draw a smooth outline over the construction, with slightly tapered fingers and only a few lines for the knuckles and the creases of the palm. Add simple curved lines for the nails, then erase the boxes and cylinders.

How to draw a fist

Start from the palm box, then curl the fingers fully: their first segments form a flat front face, almost like a second box, and the knuckles stand out on top. The thumb folds across the middle segments of the index and middle fingers.

Seen from the front, a fist shows the four knuckles and the folded fingers in steps; seen from the side, it looks like a block with the thumb on top. In a punch toward the viewer, draw it big: foreshortening makes the fist larger than the head.

Tip

Make a fist with your non-drawing hand and turn it slowly in front of you. Draw it from three angles before you draw it from imagination.

How to draw hands holding things

Draw the object first, then wrap the fingers around it. Find the points where the hand touches the object, usually the palm and the inside of the finger segments, and curl the cylinders until they meet them.

Weight changes the grip. A light object like a pen is held with the fingertips; a heavy one like a bag or a sword handle pulls the whole hand closed, with the knuckles tense. Leave a little space between the fingers and the object where they do not touch, so the grip looks real.

Male, female and stylized anime hands

The construction is the same for everyone; the proportions and the number of lines change. Male hands often get a squarer palm, thicker fingers and a few visible knuckle lines. Female hands usually get a narrower palm, long tapered fingers and almost no lines.

Children's hands are small and soft, with short fingers and a round palm, and chibi hands can be reduced to a mitten shape. Whatever the style, keep the thumb on the side of the palm: it is what makes a hand read as a hand.

Common mistakes when drawing anime hands

  • Fingers too long or too short: check that they are about as long as the palm.
  • A straight knuckle line: the knuckles form an arc, highest at the middle finger.
  • A thumb on the same line as the fingers: it starts low, near the wrist, and points away at an angle.
  • Fingers bending everywhere: they bend only at the joints and only toward the palm.
  • Hiding hands in pockets: a habit that stops you from improving. Draw them, even badly, every day.
Practice exercise

Draw 12 hands in 30 minutes from your own hand or photos: four open hands, four fists and four hands pointing or holding something. Start every one with the palm box and the finger cylinders.

Frequently asked questions

How do you draw anime hands for beginners?

Draw a box for the palm, an arc for the knuckles, a wedge for the thumb and three cylinders for each finger, then bend them for the pose and draw a smooth outline over the construction.

How long are the fingers compared with the palm?

About as long as the palm, measured on the middle finger. The middle finger is the longest and the little finger ends near the last joint of the ring finger.

How do you draw a fist in anime?

Curl the fingers fully from the palm box so their first segments form a flat front, show the knuckles on top, and fold the thumb across the index and middle fingers.

How do you draw a hand holding something?

Draw the object first, find where the palm and the fingers touch it, then curl the finger cylinders around it. Light objects are held with the fingertips, heavy ones with the whole hand.

Why are hands so hard to draw?

Because they have many parts and endless poses. Simplifying them into a palm box and finger cylinders, and drawing your own hand often, makes them much easier.
