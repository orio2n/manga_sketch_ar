How to draw anime hair, step by step

To draw anime hair, draw the head first, mark the hairline and the parting, then sketch the outer shape of the hair a little above the skull. Split that shape into a few big locks that flow away from the parting, add smaller strands and pointed tips, and darken the places where locks overlap. Think in clumps, never in single hairs.

Key takeaways

  • Draw the head first: hair sits above the skull, never glued to it.
  • Every lock flows away from the parting; the hairline tells you where the hair starts on the face.
  • Think in big locks first, then a few strands inside them, never in single hairs.
  • Spiky, short and long styles use the same steps: only the length and the direction of the locks change.

How anime hair works

Anime hair is drawn as a handful of big, solid locks instead of thousands of hairs. Each lock is a tapered shape that starts at the parting, follows the curve of the head and ends in a point or a soft curl. A few strands inside the locks and a few loose hairs at the edges are enough to suggest the texture.

The second secret is volume. Hair has thickness, so its outline sits above the skull, especially on top and at the sides. Week 5 of the SketchAI program sums it up in one tip: draw the overall shape of the hairstyle first, then separate the strands.

How to draw anime hair step by step

Follow these steps for any hairstyle. The example is short to medium hair, the most common in manga; the next sections adapt it to spiky and long hair.

  1. Step 1Draw the head

    Build the head with a sphere for the skull, a vertical axis, an eye line and the jaw. You need the skull to know where the hair sits: hair drawn without it often looks like a hat.

  2. Step 2Mark the hairline and the parting

    Draw the hairline across the forehead, a little above the eyebrows, and down in front of the ears. Then choose where the hair parts on top of the head: a point or a short line from which every lock will flow.

  3. Step 3Sketch the outer shape

    Lightly draw the silhouette of the whole hairstyle, a finger's width or more above the skull and wider at the sides. Short hair stays close to the head; thick or spiky hair goes far out. This outline is the boundary of your locks.

  4. Step 4Split it into big locks

    Divide the shape into five to nine big locks that start at the parting and wrap around the head like the slices of an orange. Draw each one as a long, tapered shape and give it a pointed or slightly rounded tip.

  5. Step 5Add the bangs

    Bring a few locks forward over the forehead. Vary their lengths, let one or two cross the eye line for a natural look, and leave gaps between them so the eyes stay readable.

  6. Step 6Add strands and split tips

    Inside the big locks, draw a few thinner strands that follow the same direction, and split some tips in two or three. Add one or two loose hairs at the edges. Stop before it gets busy: a few strands read better than many.

  7. Step 7Darken the overlaps and leave a highlight

    Press harder where one lock passes behind another, under the bangs and at the nape of the neck. For shiny hair, leave a white band across the top of the head, broken into small zigzags, and shade lightly around it.

How to draw spiky anime hair

Spiky hair is the same method with sharper locks. Draw the outer shape well above the skull, then cut it into triangular locks of different sizes: the biggest on top and at the back, the smallest around the face.

Point the spikes in a few main directions, mostly up and back, and group them in clumps of two or three. Avoid a perfect crown of identical triangles; small irregularities make spiky hair look alive instead of drawn with a ruler.

Tip

Draw each spike in one confident stroke from its base to its tip, then come back to the base with a second stroke. Slow, careful lines make spikes look wobbly.

How to draw long anime hair

Long hair starts like short hair, at the parting and the hairline, then falls. Below the ears, let the locks follow gravity and the shoulders, and draw them as long ribbons with gentle S-curves rather than straight lines.

Keep some locks in front of the shoulders and some behind, and show the hair that falls behind the neck: it gives depth to the drawing. Finish the ends with a few tapered points of different lengths, and gather them into a band for a ponytail.

Short and male anime hairstyles

Short hairstyles keep the outer shape close to the skull, so the construction shows more. A crop with small spikes, layered hair over the ears, a rounded bob and side-swept bangs are all built the same way; only the length of the locks and the parting change.

To make a character easy to recognize, give the hairstyle one strong idea: a single lock that sticks up, bangs swept to one side, an undercut. It is often the first thing people remember about a manga character.

Common mistakes when drawing anime hair

  • Hair glued to the skull: the outline sits right on the head and the character looks bald under a wig. Leave room for the volume.
  • Drawing hair by hair: hundreds of lines turn into noise. Draw big locks first and only a few strands.
  • Locks flowing in every direction: every lock should start from the parting and follow the curve of the head.
  • A straight hairline: it should curve around the forehead and come down in front of the ears.
  • Identical spikes: vary their size and direction, and group them in clumps.
Practice exercise

Draw the same head six times in 25 minutes and give it six hairstyles: spiky, short, a bob, long, a ponytail and slicked back. Start every one from the hairline and the outer shape before drawing a single lock.

Frequently asked questions

How do you draw anime hair for beginners?

Draw the head first, mark the hairline and the parting, sketch the outer shape of the hair above the skull, then divide it into big locks. Add a few strands and split tips at the end.

How do you draw spiky anime hair?

Draw the outer shape well above the skull, then cut it into triangular locks of different sizes that point mostly up and back. Group the spikes in clumps and avoid making them all the same.

How do you draw long anime hair?

Start at the parting and the hairline like short hair, then let the locks fall as long ribbons with soft S-curves over and behind the shoulders, ending in tapered points of different lengths.

Why does my anime hair look flat?

Usually because it is drawn right on the skull or line by line. Give it volume above the head, draw big locks that overlap, and darken the places where one lock passes behind another.

How do you draw the shine on anime hair?

Leave a band of white paper across the top of the head, broken into small zigzags that follow the locks, and shade lightly around it so the highlight stands out.
