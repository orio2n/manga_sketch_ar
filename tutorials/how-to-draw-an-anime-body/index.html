How to draw an anime body, step by step

To draw an anime body, build a mannequin before any outline: an oval for the head, an egg shape for the rib cage, a box for the pelvis, a curved spine between them, spheres for the joints and cylinders for the limbs. Check the proportions, then draw the body and the clothes over that construction and erase it.

  • Beginner
  • 45 min practice
  • 6 min read
  • Updated Oct 24, 2026

You'll need

  • HB pencil
  • 2B pencil
  • Eraser
  • Printer paper or a sketchbook

Key takeaways

  • Build a mannequin first: head, rib cage, pelvis, spine, joints and limbs, all as simple volumes.
  • The rib cage and the pelvis are the two big volumes: their angles decide the whole pose.
  • Use spheres for the joints and cylinders for the limbs, then hands and feet as simple blocks.
  • Draw the body and the clothes over the construction only once its proportions are right.

The mannequin method

Drawing a body from its outline is the hardest way to do it: you have to guess every curve at once. The mannequin method splits the body into a few simple volumes you can draw, check and move, like an artist's wooden mannequin.

It rests on one principle from week 3 of the SketchAI program: a character is a construction in volume before being a drawing of muscles, clothes or outlines. Once the volumes stand right, the details have somewhere to go.

Anime body proportions

Measure the body in heads: the height of the head, from the top of the skull to the chin, is your unit. Most anime styles draw adults around seven to eight heads tall, teenagers a little less, and chibis only two to three heads tall.

A few landmarks keep any figure in proportion, whatever its height. Use them as checkpoints once your mannequin is drawn.

  • The crotch sits around the middle of the body: the legs are about half of the height.
  • The elbows line up with the waist, and the wrists with the crotch.
  • The knees are halfway between the hips and the soles of the feet.
  • The shoulders are about two heads wide on an adult man, a little narrower on a woman.

How to draw an anime body step by step

Draw lightly with your HB pencil: every line of the mannequin will be erased or covered. Start with a standing pose seen from the front or at three-quarters.

  1. Step 1Mark the height and draw the head

    Mark the top of the head and the soles of the feet with two short lines, then divide the space into seven or eight heads. Draw the head as an oval at the top, with its vertical axis.

  2. Step 2Draw the rib cage

    Under the neck, draw an egg shape or a box with rounded corners, about one and a half heads tall. Draw a line down its middle: it shows which way the chest faces. You can simplify the shape to suit the style you are aiming for.

  3. Step 3Draw the pelvis

    Draw a box for the pelvis, a little narrower than the rib cage on a man and as wide or wider on a woman. Let it tilt: its front face shows the orientation of the hips, and the legs will connect to it.

  4. Step 4Connect them with the spine

    Join the rib cage and the pelvis with a curved line for the spine, then a simple waist. Check the space between the two volumes: it is what lets the torso bend, twist and lean.

  5. Step 5Add the joints and the limbs

    Place spheres at the shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees and ankles, then connect them with cylinders that taper toward the hands and feet. Keep the upper arm and the forearm about the same length, and the thigh slightly longer than the lower leg.

  6. Step 6Block in the hands and feet

    Draw each hand as a box for the palm with a few small cylinders for the fingers, and each foot as a wedge. They are small but they change the pose a lot, so place them before any detail.

  7. Step 7Draw the body and the clothes

    Draw the outline of the body over the mannequin, following the volumes: the shoulders over the spheres, the waist between the rib cage and the pelvis, the legs around the cylinders. Add the clothes on top, then erase the construction.

Male and female anime bodies

The mannequin is the same for every character; the proportions of its parts change. Many styles give men wider shoulders, a bigger rib cage, a narrower pelvis, a thicker neck and straighter lines.

Women often get narrower shoulders, a smaller rib cage, a pelvis as wide as the shoulders or wider, a slimmer waist and longer, curved lines from the waist to the thighs. Age matters too: children have a bigger head compared with the body and short limbs, and slender teens sit between the two.

How to put the mannequin in a pose

A mannequin standing straight looks stiff because real bodies never are. Tilt the rib cage and the pelvis in different directions, and the rest of the body follows: the spine curves, one shoulder drops, the weight moves onto one leg.

Practice turning the two big volumes on their own before adding the limbs. The anime poses tutorial then adds the line of action, balance and foreshortening to make poses dynamic.

Draw a body from a reference

The fastest way to understand a pose is to rebuild it. Pick a photo or a drawing of a character, look for the rib cage, the pelvis and the joints, and draw them as a mannequin next to it. Then hide the reference and draw the character from your mannequin alone.

This exercise trains your eye to see volumes instead of outlines, and it shows you which part of a pose you do not understand yet. Our guide on drawing from reference explains how to use references without copying them.

Common mistakes when drawing anime bodies

  • Starting with the outline: without a mannequin, the proportions drift and the pose looks flat. Build the volumes first.
  • A rigid torso: the rib cage and the pelvis drawn as one block. Keep a gap between them so the body can bend and twist.
  • Arms that are too short: the wrists should reach the crotch when the arms hang down.
  • Limbs drawn as flat lines: use cylinders, so they have thickness and can turn in space.
  • Joints in the wrong place: check the elbows at the waist and the knees halfway down the legs.

Frequently asked questions

How do you draw an anime body for beginners?

Use the mannequin method: draw the head, an egg shape for the rib cage, a box for the pelvis, the spine between them, spheres for the joints and cylinders for the limbs. Then draw the body and the clothes over it.

How many heads tall is an anime character?

Most styles draw adults around seven to eight heads tall and teenagers a little less. Chibis are only two to three heads tall.

What is the difference between male and female anime bodies?

Male bodies usually get wider shoulders, a bigger rib cage and a narrower pelvis; female bodies get narrower shoulders, a slimmer waist and a pelvis as wide as the shoulders or wider. The construction stays the same.

Why do my anime bodies look stiff?

Usually because the rib cage and the pelvis face the same way and the spine is straight. Tilt the two volumes in different directions and put the weight on one leg.

Can I learn to draw bodies without anatomy books?

Yes, at first. The mannequin method gives you proportions and volumes you can draw today; learn the muscles later, once your constructions stand right.
