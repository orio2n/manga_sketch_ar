How to draw anime eyes, step by step

To draw anime eyes, start with two guidelines: an eye line across the face and a vertical line through the center of each iris. Draw a thick upper lid, a short lower lid, then a tall oval iris tucked under the upper lid, a dark pupil and two white highlights. Finish with lashes and eyebrows: their angle decides the character's mood.

  • Beginner
  • 30 min practice
  • 8 min read
  • Updated Oct 24, 2026

You'll need

  • HB pencil
  • 2B or 4B pencil for dark lines
  • Eraser
  • Printer paper or a sketchbook

Key takeaways

  • The upper lid is the boldest line of an anime eye; the lower lid stays short and light.
  • Place the eyes on an eye line near the middle of the head, with about one eye's width between them.
  • The iris is a tall oval whose top hides under the upper lid. Put the highlights on the same side in both eyes.
  • The shape of the lids and the angle of the eyebrows set the personality: round for gentle, narrow and angled for cool or fierce.

What makes an eye look anime?

Anime eyes are real eyes simplified into a few strong shapes. Artists keep the lines that read from far away, like the upper lid, the iris and the highlights, and leave out most of the rest: the lower lashes, the tear duct, the small folds of skin.

They are also bigger than real eyes and often set a little lower on the face, especially for young or cute characters. That size leaves room for highlights and emotion, which is why the eyes carry so much of a character's personality.

  • A thick upper lid that often ends in a small flick at the outer corner.
  • A light, partial lower lid, often just a short line under the eye.
  • A large iris that touches or almost touches both lids.
  • One or two white highlights, placed where the light comes from.
  • Few lashes, grouped into two or three flicks at the outer corner.

Where do the eyes go on an anime face?

Before you draw a single lash, place the eyes. On a face seen from the front, draw a horizontal eye line about halfway down the head, or slightly lower for a younger character. The eyes sit on that line, with roughly the width of one eye between them.

The eye line is not flat: it wraps around the head like a ribbon around a ball. When the head turns or tilts, the line curves and both eyes follow it. The anime face tutorial shows how to build the whole head around these guidelines.

Tip

Draw guidelines with a light HB pencil and a loose wrist. You will erase them at the end, so they never need to be clean.

How to draw anime eyes step by step

Draw one eye large, about the size of your palm, so you can see every curve. Here is the eye you will draw. Once you can draw it, draw it as a pair on the same eye line.

  1. Step 1Draw two guidelines

    Draw a horizontal eye line, then a vertical line where the center of the iris will be. Press lightly: these lines only help you place the shapes, and you will erase them.

  2. Step 2Draw the upper lid

    Draw a curve that starts low at the inner corner, near the nose, rises over the iris line and comes back down at the outer corner. Make it thickest toward the outer corner and end it with a small flick. This is the boldest line of the eye, so go over it with your darker pencil.

  3. Step 3Add the lower lid

    Draw a short, thin line under the eye, curving up toward the outer corner without touching the upper lid. Leave both corners open: an anime eye looks brighter when its outline is not a closed shape.

  4. Step 4Draw the iris

    Draw a tall oval centered on the vertical guideline. Its top disappears under the upper lid and its bottom rests on the lower lid. Add a second, lighter oval inside it to mark the colored ring. A bigger iris looks younger and gentler; a smaller one looks older or more intense.

  5. Step 5Add the pupil and the highlights

    Draw a smaller oval for the pupil in the middle of the iris. Then draw two highlights: a big one near the top, overlapping the iris and the pupil, and a small one on the opposite side, near the bottom. Fill the pupil dark and leave the highlights white.

  6. Step 6Shade the top of the iris

    The upper lid casts a shadow on the eye, so darken the top of the iris with even strokes and stop in a soft curve. Leave the bottom light. That contrast between a dark top and a bright bottom is what makes anime eyes look glossy.

  7. Step 7Add the lashes, the crease and the eyebrow

    Add two or three thick lash flicks at the outer corner, a thin curved line above the lid for the crease, and the eyebrow higher up, following the curve of the eye. Erase the guidelines, then darken the lines you want to keep.

How to change the eye style for each character

Once you can draw one eye, you can draw almost any character by changing three things: the shape of the upper lid, the size of the iris and the angle of the eyebrows. Look again at the ten pairs at the top of this page: they all start from the same guidelines.

Copy each style a few times, then mix them. A sharp lid with a big iris, or a droopy lid with a small one, quickly gives you eyes that belong to your own character.

  • Round and open: a high, curved upper lid and a large iris. Gentle, young or cheerful characters.
  • Narrow and sharp: a flat upper lid that angles down toward the nose, with a smaller iris. Cool, serious or villainous characters.
  • Droopy: an upper lid that slopes down at the outer corner. Tired, relaxed or worried characters.
  • Half-closed: the upper lid covers the top half of the iris. Calm, bored or sly characters.
  • Cat-like: an angled lid and a narrow slit for the pupil. Animal-eared characters or anyone with a predatory look.

Male vs. female anime eyes

There is no strict rule, but most styles draw male characters with narrower eyes, a flatter upper lid, smaller irises and thicker, straighter eyebrows sitting close to the eyes.

Female characters often get taller eyes, a rounder upper lid, bigger irises with more highlights, longer lash flicks and thin eyebrows set higher on the forehead. Treat these as habits, not laws: a gentle boy can have big round eyes, and a fierce heroine can have sharp ones.

How to draw anime eyes at an angle or from the side

When the head turns into a three-quarter view, the eye on the far side gets narrower, because you see it at an angle across the curve of the face. The near eye keeps almost its full width, and both stay on the curved eye line.

In a side view, the eye becomes a wedge: the two lids meet at the front in a point, and the iris turns into a narrow oval. Keep the eye set back from the bridge of the nose instead of placing it at the very edge of the face. For the full method, see how to draw an anime head from any angle.

How to show emotion with the eyes and eyebrows

The eyes and eyebrows do most of the acting in a manga panel. Change the eyelids, the size of the pupils and the angle of the brows together, and the same face can laugh, cry or rage. The anime expressions tutorial adds the mouth and the rest of the face.

  • Happy: closed eyes drawn as upward arcs, eyebrows relaxed and raised.
  • Angry: eyebrows pulled down toward the nose, flat upper lids and smaller pupils.
  • Sad: the inner ends of the eyebrows raised, drooping lids and tears along the lower lid.
  • Surprised: wide-open eyes, tiny pupils with white all around, eyebrows raised high.
  • Embarrassed: eyes glancing to the side, blush lines on the cheeks, maybe a drop of sweat.
  • Determined: low, straight eyebrows and slightly narrowed lids.

Common mistakes when drawing anime eyes

Most eyes that look wrong have one of these problems. Check your drawing against the list before you darken the lines.

  • Eyes that float: drawn without an eye line, one ends up higher than the other. Always place the guidelines first.
  • Highlights that disagree: the light comes from one direction, so both eyes need their highlights on the same side.
  • A closed outline: a lower lid drawn all the way around makes the eye look stiff. Leave gaps at the corners.
  • Too much detail: lashes everywhere and the whole iris shaded dark. Keep the darkest values for the upper lid and the pupil.
  • The wrong spacing: eyes too close or too far apart. About one eye's width should fit between them.

Frequently asked questions

How do you draw anime eyes for beginners?

Start with an eye line and a vertical line for the iris, then draw the thick upper lid, a short lower lid, a tall oval iris, the pupil and two highlights. Finish with the lashes and the eyebrow, and erase the guidelines.

How far apart should anime eyes be?

On a face seen from the front, leave about the width of one eye between them. A bigger gap looks younger or cuter; a smaller gap looks more mature or intense.

Where do the highlights go in anime eyes?

Put the main highlight near the top of the iris, on the side the light comes from, and a smaller one on the opposite side near the bottom. Use the same positions in both eyes.

What is the difference between male and female anime eyes?

Male eyes are usually narrower, with flatter lids, smaller irises and thicker eyebrows. Female eyes are often taller and rounder, with bigger irises and longer lash flicks. These are style habits, not rules.

What pencil should I use to draw anime eyes?

An HB pencil for the guidelines and first lines, then a softer 2B or 4B for the upper lid, the pupil and the lashes, which are the darkest parts of the eye.
